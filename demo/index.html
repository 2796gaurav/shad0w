A real 77-intent banking table,
deciding as you type.

This table was compiled in shadow mode from the answers of a small open model (bge-small, zero-shot, 68% accurate on this task) on the public BANKING77 dataset, then certified at α = 5% on 3,000 answers it never trained on. The decision runs locally in this tab, with no server and no network after load.

Replay real traffic

Stream 800 held-out BANKING77 test messages through the table. Certified answers are served locally. The rest would go to the teacher. These 800 are a random sample of the 3,080 test messages, so their rates move a little around the test-set figures above. On a sample this small, a disagreement a bit over α is expected now and then; the bound is about the long run.
