You vs shad0w.
It decides every frame.

The right paddle is a real shad0w that learned to play Pong from an LLM's answers. It runs in your browser: no server, no network. Sixty times a second it reads the court and picks up, stay or down, in microseconds. Add balls, speed them up, shrink the paddles: it still decides in time.

Ready? Move your mouse or finger over the court, or use W/S or the arrow keys. First to 7.
