One minute of Polymarket,
read as it happens.

Press start and your browser pulls every trade on Polymarket: the last ~20,000 first, then each new one as it lands, for 60 seconds. A shad0w table reads each market's question and files it under a section and a topic in microseconds. You watch where the money goes, and every label is checked against Polymarket's own tags as you go. No server of ours is involved: your browser talks to Polymarket's public API, and nothing you see is sent anywhere.

        The minute in one paragraph

        how the labels were made

        No LLM in the loop. Polymarket's own tags taught it.

        Polymarket tags every event it lists ("Soccer", "Fed Rates", "Ukraine Map"…). We mapped those tags to 9 sections and … topics, and shad0w compiled … market questions into a … table. Then two tests: the trades of a few recorded minutes of the live feed (what this page shows you; long-running markets in it may also be in the training data), and every market in the events created in the last two weeks before the data was collected (… questions, including thousands of near-identical sports props).

        "Certified" means the table's answer passed its calibration test (it may differ from the tags on at most 5% of certified answers, with 90% confidence). Uncertified answers are still shown, marked as such. The certificate is calibrated on the mix it trained on; on the two-week set, dominated by large sports events with hundreds of prop markets each, certified answers agree less than it promises. Sections are broad; topics are finer and overlap (a Fed market tagged "Trump").

        use it on your own feed

        Any stream of short text, labelled as it arrives.

        Prediction markets are one feed. The same table works on support tickets, news wires, chat, product reviews, transactions or logs. It labels them in microseconds, on a laptop, a server or in a browser tab, without a GPU or a per-call bill. If you already have labels (tags, queues, CRM fields), shad0w compiles them directly, as here. If you don't, it learns them from your LLM's answers and takes over the cases it is certified on.

        Newsrooms and research desks

        File every incoming market, headline or filing by beat, and alert when money moves on a topic you follow.

        Risk and compliance

        Tag flows by category in real time: sports vs elections vs crypto exposure, minute by minute, with every label auditable.

        Data products

        Enrich a firehose with labels before it hits your warehouse. At microseconds a label, the labelling costs less than the network.

        Support and operations

        Route tickets, chats and alerts the moment they arrive; send only the uncertain ones to a person or an LLM.

        fine print

        What this is, and isn't.

        • Real trades. Everything comes from Polymarket's public API at the moment you press start: the latest 20,000 trades (the most it serves, roughly the last 15–30 minutes) and the live feed after that. Volumes go up and down with the hour.
        • Dollars are shares × price for each trade, as Polymarket reports them.
        • No trader is shown. The public feed includes wallets and profile names; this page never displays or stores them, and the download leaves them out.
        • The check uses each market's event tags from Polymarket, mapped with the same rules used to build the table. Tags are Polymarket's editorial choice, and a few events carry mixed tags.
        • Timing is measured in your tab: trades are labelled in blocks and the total divided, because browser clocks are too coarse to time one label.
        • Context only, not advice. shad0w is not affiliated with Polymarket.
