How it works

Your LLM keeps answering until shad0w has earned the right to.

  1. Watch

    Your LLM answers as usual. shad0w logs each question and answer.

  2. Learn

    About a thousand answers become a small table. No labels, no GPU.

  3. Prove

    On answers it never saw, it finds where it agrees with your LLM.

  4. Serve

    It answers what it proved, in microseconds. The rest go to your LLM.

Certified means shad0w differs from your LLM on at most 5% of the answers it gives, checked on held-out answers with 90% confidence. How the guarantee works

Measured, not promised.

Public datasets and a real hosted LLM. Every number is rebuilt from a saved result file.

One line of code, or none.

Wrap your LLM call, or point your client's base_url at the proxy.

pythonsupport_router.py
import shad0w

intent = shad0w.decision("intent", llm="openai/gpt-6-luna",
    options=["refund", "lost_card", "balance", "other"])

intent("my card was stolen").answer   # your LLM answers; shad0w logs it
intent.train()                        # after ~1,000 answers
intent("my card was stolen")          # Decision(answer='lost_card', source='table')

Async: await intent.adecide(text). Bring your own LLM function: shad0w.Shadow("bundle/", teacher=my_fn).

javascriptroute.mjs
import { decision } from "shad0wllm";

const intent = await decision("intent", {
  options: ["refund", "lost_card", "balance", "other"],
  llm: "openai/gpt-6-luna", bundle: "shad0w/intent/bundle" });

const d = await intent.decide("my card was stolen");
// { answer: "lost_card", source: "table", certified: true }

Questions

Is this just a cache?

No. A cache only answers texts it has seen before. shad0w answers new wordings of a question it has learned, and its guarantee covers new messages that look like your traffic.

What if my LLM is wrong?

Then shad0w is wrong in the same way. The guarantee is agreement with your LLM, not with the truth. For a bound against the truth, certify on a few hundred human-labelled examples instead.

Can a 90% promise miss?

Yes, sometimes. In our runs shad0w differed 3.0–6.0% of the time against a 5% limit. That is why live spot checks keep comparing it with your LLM after launch.

What does it cost?

Nothing. It is Apache-2.0 open source. The Python runtime needs only numpy; the JavaScript and C runtimes need nothing. Your data and logs stay on your machine.
