how it works

Watch. Train. Certify.
Then get out of the way.

No labelling, no GPU, no new model to babysit. Your LLM is the teacher; shad0w is the student that only answers when it can prove it will agree.

STEP 1 · simulated

Watch

Your LLM keeps answering. shad0w writes each question and answer down.

Example: {"text": "block my card", "intent": "lost_card"}: one line per decision, in a plain file you own.
STEP 2 · simulated

Train

About 1,000 answers become a hashed word table with 8-bit weights, in seconds to a minute on a laptop.

1,000 answers0%
Example: intent.train() or shad0w train --log log.jsonl → a 1.5 MB file.
STEP 3 · simulated

Certify & serve

On answers it never saw, shad0w finds the confidence above which it disagrees with your LLM on at most α (5% by default), with 90% confidence. Below that line it does not answer.

CERTIFIED
≤ 5% · 90%
Above the line: the table answers in µs. Below it: your LLM answers, and the answer is logged so the table learns.

the numbers

Measured, not promised.

Public datasets, real teacher models, the certificate at α = 5%. A sample of served answers is re-asked to the LLM live; the rest rests on the certificate. The source, teacher, hardware and date sit under every chart. Full tables: benchmarks & evidence.

Thousands of times faster

time per decision, log scale

A decision costs 1.0 µs in C, 10 µs in JavaScript and 2.4 µs in Python (5.2 µs with per-option probabilities). A small local decision model takes 271 ms; a hosted LLM 1416 ms.

Most traffic never needs the LLM

share of traffic answered by the table, by task

On 3 intent and topic tasks the table took 61–86% of the traffic (teacher: gpt-4.1-mini (OpenAI)), with realised disagreement 3.0–6.0%, and end-to-end accuracy within 1 point of the LLM alone.

It gets better as you use it

share answered by the table at α = 5%, by how many LLM answers were logged

Every answer your LLM gives is new training data. On BANKING77 the table's share grew from 0% to 60% as logged traffic went from 250 to 10,000.

Your whole app gets faster

The table answers the easy majority instantly and the LLM handles the rest, so the average drops from 382 ms to 115 ms (measured end to end), while end-to-end accuracy moved +1.0 points.

tune it

One knob that matters.
A few that help.

α is the share of served answers that may differ from your LLM. Pick it per question; shad0w finds the confidence threshold that proves it. Everything else has a sensible default.

α · max disagreement with your LLM
–of BANKING77 traffic answered by the table
–realised disagreement on those answers

alpha = 0.05max disagreement on served answers. Lower is safer and saves fewer calls.
delta = 0.1the chance the certificate itself is unlucky: 90% confidence.
mode = "shadow"compute every answer, return your LLM's, and report what you would have saved. Zero risk.
canary = 0.1let the table answer 10% of the certified traffic first.
never_serve = [...]labels that always go to your LLM, whatever the confidence.
min_confidencea manual floor on top of the certificate. It can only make the table stricter.
audit_rate = 0.01share of decisions re-asked to your LLM, so the dashboard shows live agreement.
auto_train = 1000retrain and re-certify every 1,000 new answers; a worse table never replaces a better one.

Set them in code, as SHAD0W_* environment variables or in shad0w.toml. Every setting, explained → · shadow → canary → serve

honest limits

What it won't do.

shad0w is strong engineering on well-known ideas, not magic. Here is where it stops.

Generate textIt picks one answer from your list. Open-ended chat, summaries and reasoning stay with your LLM.
Read between the linesSarcasm, tone and sentiment top out near 79%. shad0w then certifies 0% and defers everything, as it should.
Beat your LLMThe guarantee is agreement with your LLM, not the truth. A wrong LLM makes a wrong table. Calibrate on ~300 human labels for a bound against the truth.
Ignore changeThe bound holds for traffic like the traffic it was certified on. The drift guard and spot checks tell you when to retrain.
faq

Questions people ask

Is this just a cache?

No. A cache only answers texts it has seen before. shad0w generalises: it answers new wordings of a question it has learned, and its guarantee covers new texts drawn like your traffic. Exact repeats are free on top.

What does "certified" actually mean?

On answers it never trained on, shad0w picks the lowest confidence threshold for which it can prove, using Learn-then-Test with Clopper–Pearson bounds, that among the answers it serves, disagreement with your LLM is at most α (default 5%) with 90% confidence. Below the threshold it doesn't answer. Your LLM does. The 10% is real: in earlier runs the realised rate landed above α on 2 of 7 tasks (3.9–5.7% (above 5% on 2 of 7 tasks; max over seeds 6.5%)), which is why live spot checks exist.

How many examples does it need?

Around 1,000 logged answers per question for a useful start (100 is the hard minimum). The share it can take keeps growing with more traffic: 0% at 250 answers and 60% at 10,000 in our BANKING77 run.

Does it work with the OpenAI Decisions API, Jev, Kev or Laya?

Yes, and with no code change. Those requests already carry the question name and the allowed answers, so shad0w proxy intercepts /v1/decisions and /v1/systemone on its own: certified questions are answered from the table in the same JSON shape, the rest go upstream and are logged. Any of them can also be the teacher (llm="openai-decisions/gpt-6-luna", llm="systemone/kev-0.8b").

What can I tune?

α (how much disagreement you accept), δ, the rows needed before training, the spot-check rate, shadow mode, a canary share, labels that must always go to the LLM, and a manual confidence floor that can only make the table stricter. In code, as environment variables, or in shad0w.toml. See configuration.

Does my data leave my machine?

No. Logs, training and the table are all local files. The only network calls are the LLM calls you already make.

What does it cost?

Nothing. It is Apache-2.0 open source. The runtime needs only numpy (Python) or nothing at all (JavaScript, C).

Which LLMs work as the teacher?

Any of them. Built-in presets cover OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Groq, Together, OpenRouter, Mistral, DeepSeek, Fireworks, xAI, Ollama, vLLM and LM Studio, and any other OpenAI-compatible URL. You can also pass a plain function, or LiteLLM, LangChain or the Vercel AI SDK.

How is it different from fine-tuning a small model?

No GPU, no labelling, no training pipeline, microseconds instead of milliseconds, and a guarantee instead of a hope. Fine-tuning wins on tasks that need real language understanding; shad0w wins on the high-volume, recognisable ones and defers the rest.
