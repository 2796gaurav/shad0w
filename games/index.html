games and live demos · shad0w at full speed

Fast enough to play.

A game gives every decision a few milliseconds. shad0w learned each of these from an LLM's answers, then plays where speed decides who wins: two you can play in your browser, two recorded races against LLMs and System One decision models. And one that isn't a game: a live minute of Polymarket, every trade labelled as it lands.

new · live · real money

One minute of Polymarket, read live

Press start: your browser reads every Polymarket trade for 60 seconds, and shad0w files each one by section and topic in microseconds, checked live against Polymarket's own tags. Download the result.

The snake arena

shad0w, three System One models on an Apple M5 Pro's GPU, and a hosted LLM play the same games at up to 120 moves a second. shad0w scores 28.0 at every speed; the LLM drops from 25.9 to 0.1 at 10 moves a second.
