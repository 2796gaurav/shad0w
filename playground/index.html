playground · try shad0w yourself

Don't take our word for it. Try it.

Everything here runs in your browser or replays a real recording: a live feed of real money, a prompt guard, a web agent that reads raw HTML, a drawing game that reads pixels, a box you can type into, two games you can play and two races against LLMs and System One decision models. Each shows the same thing: decisions in microseconds, text or images, with a certificate that says when to trust them.
