Benchmarks & evidence#

Every figure on this site comes from numbers.json, which the benchmark kit generates from result files. This page lists them with their sources. Hardware: Apple M5 Pro (15 cores, 24 GB) unless a row says otherwise.

How to read these numbers#

  • α is the bound you ask for: the table may disagree with your LLM on at most α of the answers it serves. δ is the chance the certificate is wrong because of an unlucky calibration sample; the default δ = 0.1 means 90% confidence.
  • Answered by the table (served share) is the share of fresh traffic the table answered without the LLM.
  • Realised disagreement is measured on fresh traffic, after certification. It can exceed α in up to δ of certifications. It did on 2 of 7 earlier tasks: 3.9–5.7% (above 5% on 2 of 7 tasks; max over seeds 6.5%).
  • Agreement is not accuracy. The bound is against your LLM. Accuracy against gold labels is reported separately.

Where shad0w loses#

  • SST-2: 0% served; the table tops out near 79%.
  • LEDGAR: 13% served.
  • CLINC150: realised disagreement above α, because the traffic mix moved. CLINC150's test split has far more out-of-scope messages than its training split, and the training split is what the log looks like here. The certificate is computed on the logged mix, so it was certified on mostly in-scope traffic and then served a test set full of off-topic messages. Realised disagreement on served answers went over α on every seed. Some out-of-scope messages were answered with a real intent: 5% of them. This is the "traffic like the calibration traffic" condition failing, not a bug. When we calibrated on a mix that looks like the test traffic, the bound held again at a lower served share. In a live deployment the drift guard flags this shift after a few hundred decisions and sends those answers to the LLM; benchmark 6 compares methods without the guard, so it shows the unguarded number. What to do: log off-topic traffic at its real rate, always offer an other option, and retrain when the off-topic share changes (limits).
