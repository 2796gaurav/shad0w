Roadmap#

Ordered by how much each item helps people use shadow mode safely. Open an issue to push something up the list.

Next#

  • shad0w audit: a CLI over shad0w.audit.Auditor. It reads a stream of served decisions plus spot checks and reports the live, anytime-valid bound.
  • Compile report: one HTML page per bundle. It shows agreement, offload by α, a confidence histogram and class balance, and warns when calibration traffic looks unlike recent traffic.
  • Class-conditional (Mondrian) thresholds, so a class-mix shift cannot hide behind a marginal bound.
  • Async wrapper (AsyncShadow) for asyncio teachers.

Later#

  • Go and Rust bindings for the C core, and a WASM build.
  • An optional "plus" tier: the hashed table plus a small static-embedding block, measured at about +1.7 points for +25–50 µs.
  • Ready-made adapters for LiteLLM, LangChain/LangGraph and agent-SDK pre-tool hooks.

Not planned#

  • Zero-shot answering without a teacher or logs. The measured quality is too low to be useful.
  • Sentiment and tone in the compact table. That is a measured representation ceiling.

Something unclear? Open an issue or suggest an edit.
