FAQ#

Is this a cache?#

No. A cache answers only texts it has seen. shad0w generalises to new wordings of what it learned, and its guarantee covers new texts drawn like your traffic.

How much traffic does it take over?#

On seven public intent and topic tasks, 45–71% at α = 5%, with end-to-end accuracy within 1 point of the LLM alone. It grows with logged traffic: 31% at 2k answers and 70% at 100k on AG News. Sentiment-like tasks: near 0%, by design.

How fast is it?#

About 1 µs per decision in C, 7–11 µs in JavaScript, and 8–15 µs in Python. Through the proxy or HTTP server, add the network hop (~0.2 ms on localhost).

Does it need a GPU, labels or a training pipeline?#

No. It needs a CPU, your LLM's answers, and seconds of training.

Which LLMs can be the teacher?#

Any. Presets cover OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Groq, Together, OpenRouter, Mistral, DeepSeek, Fireworks, xAI, Azure, Ollama, vLLM and LM Studio. Anything else works through base_url=, a complete= function, or a plain fn(text) -> answer.

Does any data leave my machine?#

No. Logs, training and tables are local files. The only network traffic is the LLM calls you already make.

What happens when my LLM changes (new model, new prompt)?#

The certificate is against the old teacher. Clear or rotate the log, let the new LLM answer for a while, and retrain. Until then, keep the old table (it is certified against the old LLM) or remove it to send everything to the new one.

Off-topic messages get a real intent. Why?#

The table can only answer with one of your options. Add a catch-all such as "other": "anything else". Your LLM then labels off-topic traffic other, the table learns that, and off-topic messages stop landing in real intents.

Can I use it for multi-label or open-ended output?#

No. One answer from a fixed list per question. For several labels, use one yes/no question per label.

How big is the table?#

About 2 MB for 77 options. Smaller is possible: 256 KB still kept 88.5% of the share answered in our tests.

Is it safe against adversarial input?#

Use exposed=True for user-facing, adversarial channels. The table then also defers any answer that a few inserted words could flip (an exact robustness radius). For high-stakes decisions, keep your LLM in the loop.

What does it cost?#

Nothing. Apache-2.0.

Where do I report a problem or share results?#

On GitHub. Results on your own data, good or bad, are very welcome.
